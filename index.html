Frequently Asked Questions

What is KBN University & NIAT India?
Khaja Bandanawaz (KBN) University is a Category-1 UGC recognised university offering 200+ degree programs across Medicine, AI Engineering, Law, Pharmacy & Nursing in collaboration with NIAT India industry cohorts.
Where is the KBN University campus located?
The main green university campus is located at Rauza-i Buzurg, Kalaburagi, Karnataka 585104.
What are the eligibility criteria for MBBS, B.Tech & MBA?
MBBS requires 10+2 PCB (50%+) and NEET UG qualification. B.Tech requires 10+2 PCM (50%+) or JEE Main score. MBA requires graduation in any discipline with 50%+ marks.
How can I apply for Admissions 2026-27 online?
Click on the red "Apply Now" button in the top menu or complete the Quick Admission Enquiry form to get instant counselor guidance.
What is the NAT Assessment Test and scholarship criteria?
NAT (National Aptitude Test) evaluates problem-solving, logical reasoning, and technical aptitude. High scorers qualify for up to 100% tuition fee scholarships.
How do I attempt the online NAT test?
Upon registration, an online test link with your login credentials will be emailed to you. You can attempt the test from home using a webcam.
What makes the KBN & NIAT upskilling program special?
Our program features hands-on real-world AI projects, mentorship by top product engineers, clinical rotations, and placement support with 2500+ hiring companies.
Are real-world AI projects included in the curriculum?
Yes! Every student completes 10+ industry-grade projects in Full Stack Web Development, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Robotics.
What is the average stipend and placement support provided?
NIATians have secured over ₹2.92 Crore in internship stipends with highest CTC packages reaching up to ₹57 LPA.
Which top companies hire from KBN & NIAT cohorts?
Hiring partners include Amazon, D.E. Shaw, Meesho, Walmart, Accenture, Prodapt, Deloitte, and 2500+ global tech recruiters.
