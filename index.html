Frequently Asked Questions

What is SGT University & NIAT India?
SGT University is a NAAC A+ accredited state private university in Gurugram offering 200+ degree programs across Medicine, Engineering, Law, Pharmacy & Nursing in collaboration with NIAT India upskilling cohorts.
What makes the SGT & NIAT upskilling program special?
Our program features hands-on real-world AI projects, mentorship by industry experts, direct clinical rotations at the 800-bed SGT hospital, and placement support with 2500+ hiring companies.
Where is the SGT University campus located?
The 100-acre green campus is located at Chandu-Budhera, Gurugram-Badli Road, Gurugram, Haryana 122505, near Delhi-NCR.
What are the eligibility criteria for MBBS, B.Tech & MBA?
MBBS requires 10+2 PCB (50%+) and NEET UG qualification. B.Tech requires 10+2 PCM (50%+) or JEE Main score. MBA requires graduation in any discipline with 50%+ marks.
