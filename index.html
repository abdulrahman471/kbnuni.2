Know SGT University

Founded with a vision of global educational excellence, Shree Guru Gobind Singh Tricentenary (SGT) University stands at the intersection of multidisciplinary applied education and translational research. With 19 specialized faculties across Gurugram, Delhi-NCR, SGT empowers students to discover their passion through global perspectives, industry collaborations, and holistic development.

Vision

To be an exceptional knowledge-driven institution advancing a culture of honesty and compassion.

Mission

Fostering diligence, innovation, entrepreneurship, collaboration, and community service.
